# Team Project Charter

## 1. Goals

### Good Result
We are looking to have a complete and polished final result. The end product should be something we would be proud to present after class. Of course, it is also important to all of us that we receive an A on the project and that everyone collaborates properly and respectfully.

### What We Want Out of This
We want to learn more about the case and understand the practical application of this project. On top of improving our coding skills, we hope to come away with a better understanding of how businesses use data science to make critical decisions.

### Time Limitations
Our time will mainly be limited by other classes and extracurricular activities. We will strive to complete as much work in class as possible and avoid working on weekends when possible. A full table of availability is in [Section 4](#4-how-we-work-together).

### Limiting a Bad Experience
A bad experience with this project would be characterized by us not achieving the good result established above. This would mean delivering a sloppy project, taking shortcuts to avoid learning, or poor collaboration between team members. To avoid this, we will communicate clearly with each other and hold each other accountable if something is not at the level it should be.

### Disagreements
The person who owns the task has final authority if we cannot come to an agreement. In all other cases, a discussion and group vote should take place. If a majority cannot be reached, the task owner again has final authority.

### Unreachable Member
1. **After 1 day:** send a message checking in.
2. **After 2 days:** reach out again, and hold a team discussion to decide how to split the missing work.
3. **After 3 days:** contact Professor Williamson.
4. **Within 24 hours of a deadline:** the team will meet to split up the missing member's work, and Professor Williamson will be notified after the problem has been resolved.
